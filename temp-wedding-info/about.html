Our Love Story

How Alexandra and James Met and Fell in Love

Alexandra and James first met in the summer of 2020 at a mutual friend's backyard barbecue. What started as a casual conversation about their shared love of hiking and travel quickly blossomed into something deeper.

Over the next year, they explored countless trails together, from the local foothills to distant mountain ranges. Their adventures took them to national parks, coastal getaways, and even overseas to experience new cultures and cuisines.

In the winter of 2021, during a snowy weekend getaway to a cozy cabin in the mountains, James knew he wanted to spend the rest of his life with Alexandra. He proposed by the fireplace, surrounded by the warmth of their love and the crackling of the fire.

Now, they are excited to share their special day with family and friends as they embark on this new chapter together.
