Location

Where We Celebrate

The Leela Palace Bengaluru stands as a beacon of luxury and elegance on Old Airport Road. The Maharaja Ballroom, with its soaring ceilings, crystal chandeliers, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow, provides the perfect canvas for our celebration.

With a separate entrance from Gate 6, dedicated valet parking, and exclusive access to both the indoor ballroom and the outdoor lawn, our events will enjoy complete privacy and grandeur.

Venue Highlights

Everything You Need for a Perfect Celebration

Maharaja Ballroom

Exclusive use of the entire Maharaja Ballroom (divisible into Maharaja 1 & 2) with separate entrance from Gate 6, dedicated lobby, and valet parking. Indoor + outdoor spaces included.

Getting Ready Rooms

2 dedicated getting-ready rooms (bride & groom) included in the package. Separate from guest room block.

Catering & Dining

2× high tea + lunch (50 pax), dinner with drinks (100 pax). 4 veg starters, 5 live stations, premium bar, 3 desserts + ice cream, chaat counter. Full menu customization available.

Entertainment Ready

Built-in sound system, stage lighting, DJ booth area. Music licensing handled by venue. Perfect for sangeet, reception, and dancing.

Modern Amenities

High-speed WiFi, climate control, backup power, wheelchair accessible, dedicated event coordination team on-site.

Parking & Access

Dedicated valet parking at Gate 6 entrance. Easy access from Old Airport Road. Drop-off zone for elderly guests.
