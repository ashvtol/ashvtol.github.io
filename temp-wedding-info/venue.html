Once you arrive at The Leela Palace

Whichever route you travel, arrival at the venue works the same way. Ask your driver for Gate 6, the event gate for the Maharaja Ballroom. Valet parking is available at the event entrance, and there is a drop-off point right by the door for anyone who would prefer a shorter walk.
